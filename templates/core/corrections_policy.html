Corrections Policy

{{ SITE_NAME }} takes correction requests seriously. Our goal is to keep published information accurate, clear, and fair.

How To Request a Correction

Please send the article URL, the specific issue, and supporting information through the contact page. Requests with documents, official links, or direct evidence can be reviewed faster.

What We May Do

Depending on the review, we may correct a factual error, add clarification, update outdated information, adjust a headline, add source context, or remove content that cannot be responsibly maintained.

Transparency

When a meaningful correction is made, the article may include a correction note or updated timestamp so readers can understand what changed.
