Fact-Checking Policy

{{ SITE_NAME }} follows a practical verification process before publishing public-interest news and updates.

Verification Steps

Editors review names, dates, places, claims, official references, and supporting material. For sensitive topics such as crime, health, finance, legal matters, government schemes, elections, and public safety, we prefer official sources or clearly attributed statements.

Unconfirmed Information

If a detail cannot be independently verified at the time of publication, it should be identified as unconfirmed, attributed to the available source, or excluded until stronger confirmation is available.

Reader Reports

Readers can send corrections or supporting documents through the contact page. We review credible requests and update articles when the record requires clarification.
